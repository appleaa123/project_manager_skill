Subject: User research findings — onboarding assumptions may be off

Sam,

Wrapped the 8 moderated sessions on the onboarding prototype this week. Wanted to flag before
sprint review: 6 of 8 participants got confused by the "connect your bank account" step being
screen 2 — they expected to see the app's value (budget dashboard preview) before being asked to
link financial accounts. Two participants dropped off at that exact screen in the test.

This contradicts the assumption we scoped the redesign around (that users want to connect
accounts immediately for a personalized setup). It might mean screen 2 and 3 need to be reordered,
or we need an interstitial "here's what you'll get" screen before the account-linking step.

Not saying we throw out the sprint plan, but I don't think we can ship the current flow order as
final without addressing this. Want to find 20 min before review?
